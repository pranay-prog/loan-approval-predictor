Preprocessing pipeline

  1. Load applicant CSV (income, credit history, loan amount…)
  2. Feature engineering — total income, EMI, loan-to-income
  3. Impute numeric — median (SimpleImputer)
  4. Impute categorical — most frequent value
  5. Encode categorical columns — OneHotEncoder
  6. Scale numeric (Logistic Regression only)
  7. Train Decision Tree / Logistic Regression · 80/20 split · 5-fold CV
